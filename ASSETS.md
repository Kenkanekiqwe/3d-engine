# CryHorror external assets

## Environment

- Poly Haven Pine Forest collection — CC0. Forest-focused collection with trees and nature assets.
- Kenney Nature Kit — CC0. 330 nature assets including trees, rocks and foliage.
- Poly Haven forest-ground materials — CC0.

## Audio

- OpenGameArt CC0 Audio collection: forest ambience, footsteps on wood/stone/leaves/gravel/mud, horror loops and SFX.
- OpenGameArt Ambient Horror — CC0, WAV/OGG.
- Freesound Dark Horror Forest Soundscape — CC0.

## Import policy

Binary third-party packs are downloaded by the bootstrap script rather than blindly committed into the source tree. License files remain with extracted content.

`assets/external` is for third-party content. `assets/generated` is for generated/build output.
